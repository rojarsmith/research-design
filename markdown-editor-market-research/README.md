# Market room for a paid Markdown editor

Research date: 2026-11-03. The full report is in [README.zh-TW.md](README.zh-TW.md); this page is a short overview. For billing and licensing, see [desktop-licensing-research](../desktop-licensing-research/README.md).

## Summary

- A general-purpose paid Markdown editor has little room left. It competes with free tools (Obsidian, VS Code, Zettlr, MarkText, Notion) and with established cheap paid apps: Typora at $14.99 one-time, iA Writer, Bear at about $2.99/month, and Ulysses at about $5.99/month.
- New editors keep launching in 2026, some at $3.99. Third-party estimates put most indie Markdown apps under $10K/month. These figures are unaudited.
- Room remains in niches where free tools fall short:
  - team and enterprise licensing (Typora offers no bulk licenses)
  - specific output formats such as Taiwan government documents, theses and manuals
  - CJK typesetting
  - AI agent workflows
  - docs-as-code for non-engineers
- Validate before building. Start with a landing page and Stripe pre-orders; the suggested threshold is 30–50 pre-orders or paid intents in two to four weeks. Price individuals at $20–40 one-time or about $5/month, and teams per seat per year.

None of these niches has been validated with interviews or pre-orders.
