## 1. 臺北高等行政法院 113年度訴字第948號 (2024-12-19): appeal?

**Result: still unverified (no appeal found). The first-instance facts are confirmed and expanded.**

Confirmed facts (primary, the full judgment text):
- Court: 臺北高等行政法院, 第一庭. Case 113年度訴字第948號. Judgment date 113-12-19 (2024-12-19).
- Plaintiff: 全興食品股份有限公司, a company newly created by a split (分割) from 全興國際水產股份有限公司 under the 企業併購法. Defendant: 財政部北區國稅局.
- Tax: 109年度 (2020) 營利事業所得稅. The plaintiff reported a 處分資產損失 of NT$328,540,917 on selling its 100% stake in 宇昌生物科技有限公司. The NTA allowed only NT$118,130,331 and disallowed NT$210,410,586.
- Key figures: original investment cost NT$546,390,000. Book value at the split, NT$337,368,402 (about NT$3.37億), was used as the tax cost.
- Ruling basis applied: 財政部96年10月3日台財稅字第09604545320號令. When a company splits off a business under the 企併法 to a 100%-owned subsidiary, the book value of the assets split off is treated as the actual transaction price (實際成交價格). The court held this ruling consistent with the law. It also held that the investment loss counted as realized in the split year (107年度) and could not be deducted under 企併法第44條第3項. A separate 96年10月31日 ruling on carrying over temporary differences did not apply.
- Outcome: 原告之訴駁回 (the plaintiff's claim was dismissed).
- Earlier stages: 復查決定 2024-01-11 (北區國稅法務字第1130000523號). 訴願決定 2024-06-13 (台財法字第11313915680號).

Appeal search (all negative):
- I searched for 全興食品 together with 最高行政法院 and 114年度上字 in standard and extended modes, and ran site searches on judgment.judicial.gov.tw. No Supreme Administrative Court (TPAA) decision naming 全興食品 or 宇昌 was indexed.
- The judgment's 歷審裁判 (case history) page redirected to an error page and could not be read. The keyword query on the 司法院 FJUD system cannot be scripted through GET requests.
- Supreme Administrative Court cases that did appear (113上616 永豐餘, 113上734, 113上475, 113上131) are unrelated.
- Conclusion: no evidence of an appeal decision as of 2026-11-20. This does not prove no appeal was filed. An appeal may be pending, or may have been dismissed by an unindexed 裁定. To close this, run a manual 司法院 FJUD search on the party name "全興食品" in the 最高行政法院 court filter.

## 2. 企業併購法 amendment on share-exchange tax deferral (產業控股公司 換股緩課)

**Result: corrected. The amendment was not promulgated as of mid-November 2026. The belief that it passed third reading around August 2026 is not supported.**

Timeline:
- 2025-08-21: the 行政院 approved the draft amending 企業併購法 第44條之2, 第52條之1 and 第54條, which was sent to the 立法院. [Primary] https://www.ey.gov.tw/Page/9277F759E41CCD91/cd856b89-d613-41de-a3c5-3e9bbc729d29
- 2025-11-10: the 立法院經濟委員會 completed its committee review (初審). The penalty clause (第52條之1) was held for party negotiation (保留送協商). [Secondary] CNA https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aipl/202511100212.aspx
- 2026-04-16: party-caucus negotiation (黨團協商) was reported complete. The bill was waiting to be scheduled for a plenary session. [Secondary] 工商時報 https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260418700704-430503
- 2026-07-21: 經濟日報 reported that the caucuses agreed to pass the 行政院 version. The bill only needed to be put on the plenary agenda. [Secondary] https://money.udn.com/money/story/6710/9639872
- 2026-08-21: caucus negotiation set the 企業併購法 for third reading at the plenaries on 8/25 or 8/28. [Secondary] https://udn.com/news/story/6656/9705721
- 2026-08-25: 經濟日報 wrote "企業併購法預計本周內三讀" ("expected to pass third reading this week"). [Secondary] https://money.udn.com/money/story/7307/9711761
- 2026-08-27: the 8/28 plenary was moved up to 8/27 because of a typhoon. It was the last plenary day of the session. The 產業創新條例 amendment and the 無人機/無人載具 條例 passed third reading that day. No report found says the 企業併購法 passed. [Secondary] https://cna.com.tw/news/aipl/202608270256.aspx ; https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aipl/202608270207.aspx ; https://udn.com/news/story/6656/9718186
- Primary check, 全國法規資料庫: the 企業併購法 amendment history lists only 2002, 2004, 2015 and 2022-06-15. The page says its legislative data is current through 115/11/13 (2026-11-13). The current text has no 第44條之2 or 第52條之1, and 第54條 still reads "本法自公布後六個月施行。" [Primary] https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawHistory.aspx?pcode=J0080041 ; https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCODE=J0080041
- Primary cross-check: the database's list of new laws shows bills passed in the same week were promulgated. The 無人載具條例 appears on 115-09-11 and the 產業創新條例 on 115-09-30. No 企業併購法 entry appears. [Primary] https://law.moj.gov.tw/News/NewsList.aspx?type=l
- Conclusion: the bill most likely missed third reading at the end of the session on 2026-08-27 and carried over to the session that began in Sept 2026. I found no report of passage in Sept to Nov 2026. Status: **pending in the 立法院**. There is no effective date.

Draft content (行政院版, as reviewed by committee; secondary unless noted):
- 第44條之2: applies when a company becomes a 100%-owned subsidiary through a share exchange (股份轉換) under 企併法第29條, and the acquiring company is recognized by 國發會 as an 產業控股公司 [primary: EY page]. The shareholders' 證券交易所得 from the exchange may be excluded from that year's 基本所得額 (the alternative minimum tax base). It is taxed instead when the holding-company shares are actually transferred, or when they enter a 有價證券保管劃撥帳戶 (securities custody account).
- Recognition procedure: apply to 國發會 by January 31 of the year after the exchange. 國發會 replies by March 31. The acquired company then files with its tax office within 15 days of the notice. [Secondary, CNA 2025-11-10]
- Sunset: the deferral applies only to exchanges within 5 years after the article takes effect. Affiliated companies (關係企業) are excluded. Detailed conditions are left to secondary regulations. [Secondary: KPMG 2025-12 https://kpmg.com/tw/zh/media/press-releases/2025/12/draft-amendments-insights-from-kpmg1.html ; 工商時報 2026-02-09 https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260209700713-431204]
- 第52條之1 penalty: NT$50,000 to 500,000 for late or false filing, and NT$100,000 to 1,000,000 if the filing is still not corrected after an order. [Secondary, CNA; 經濟日報 2026-07-21]
- Secondary regulation: 國發會 and 財政部 are to issue a 產業控股公司認定辦法 (holding-company recognition rules). Per 國發會, the rules would cover the holding company's size, type and scope of business. No pre-announcement of a draft was found. 國發會 targets 15 產業控股公司 within 5 years. [Primary: 國發會 https://www.ndc.gov.tw/nc_27_39283]
- Effective date: reports say the new articles take effect on a date set by the 行政院 (未定). This is moot until the bill is promulgated.
- Relevance to this study: even after passage, the deferral covers only domestic §29 share exchanges into a 國發會-recognized 產業控股公司. It does not cover a spin-off (分割) or a flip to a foreign parent.

## 3. US flip (Taiwan startup under a US/Cayman holdco by share exchange)

**Result: new evidence, partial. The legal basis and approvals are documented. No Taiwan source gives a step-by-step procedure, timeline or cost figure. Costs remain unverified.**

Legal basis (primary):
- 企業併購法 第31條第3項: "公司與外國公司進行股份轉換時，準用前二項、前二條及第二十一條規定。" This confirms the known basis. It is the current article number under the law as amended to 2022-06-15. Article 21 conditions: the foreign company must be a company limited by shares or a limited company that its governing law allows to merge; it must approve the deal by a valid resolution under its own law; the surviving or new company must be a company limited by shares (股份有限公司); and it must appoint a 送達代收人 (agent for service of process) in Taiwan. Under 第29條, the shareholder resolution needs a 2/3 quorum and a majority vote. [Primary] https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCODE=J0080041
- Note: 經濟部 letters say the old 第31條 provisions moved to the current 第34條 after the 2015 full revision. Check which article a cited 函釋 refers to. [Secondary pointer] https://www.rootlaw.com.tw/LetterContent.aspx?LCode=14&SOID=200626

Approvals (mixed):
- Inbound: a foreign holdco acquiring 100% of a Taiwan company is a foreign investment (華僑及外國人投資). It needs approval from the 經濟部投資審議司 (DIR, formerly 投審會) under the 外國人投資條例. Deloitte (2021) says cross-border share exchanges need DIR approval before closing. DIR review now looks closely at ultimate beneficial owners, PRC (陸資) links, critical-industry and national-security issues, and technology access. KPMG's e-Tax Alert 222 (2026-08-19) says review density is rising and timelines lengthening. [Secondary] https://meet.bnext.com.tw/articles/view/47546 ; https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/tw/pdf/2026/08/e-tax-alert-issue-222.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf
- DIR guidance on foreign-company shares as contributed capital (出資種類): a foreign company's shares can be used through 企併法 M&A or a 公司法 share exchange only if the company (a) has real operations, for example over one year of operation, premises and staff, or two or more operating subsidiaries; (b) is listed outside the PRC; or (c) is in the same group, with over 90% held directly or indirectly. Indirect holding brings extra conditions such as a 五加二 industry, accumulated losses and a cash capital increase. A newly formed Delaware or Cayman shell usually fails (a) and (b), so (c) or a different route is needed. The DIR file seen is the 106.08.25 (2017) version. The current version was not confirmed (403 on fetch). [Primary origin, seen via search snippet] https://www.moea.gov.tw/Mns/dir/investment/wHandDirApply_File.ashx?file_id=33 ; a blog makes the same point: https://yylallen.substack.com/p/194 (2025-05-02)
- Outbound: a Taiwan company investing abroad (for example a corporate shareholder taking holdco shares) falls under 產業創新條例第22條 and the 公司國外投資處理辦法. Above NT$15億 it needs prior approval. At or below that, it files with DIR after investing. The 2025-05-07 amendment adds prior approval for specific countries, regions, industries or technologies regardless of amount, and penalties up to NT$1,000萬 (2025 law-firm note; check current status). Per Tayhuan (2025-10-14) the amendment was not yet in force pending a 行政院 date. Individual founders are not subject to the corporate outbound-investment rules. That last point is my inference and needs a source. [Secondary] https://tayhuan.com/2025/10/14/%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8%E5%9C%8B%E5%A4%96%E6%8A%95%E8%B3%87%E6%96%B0%E8%A6%8F%E7%AF%84/
- Later funding: foreign VCs investing in the offshore holdco need no DIR approval (PRC investors aside), because the investment is not in a Taiwan company. This is a main reason offshore (Cayman/BVI/Singapore) structures are preferred. [Secondary, Lee and Li, Chambers VC 2026] https://chambers.com/practice-guides/guides/venture-capital-2026/taiwan/law-and-practice

Tax and other frictions (secondary):
- Taiwan individuals' gains on unlisted shares count toward 基本所得額. Foreign shareholders should consider 證券交易稅. The Taiwan target's dividends to a non-treaty parent face 21% withholding. US-parent dividends face 30% withholding without a treaty. [Deloitte 2021, above]
- If the Taiwan operating company later uses a KY holdco to list in Taiwan, the authorities may treat it as an indirect listing (迂迴上市). CFC rules apply to low-tax holdcos. Foreign employee ESOP exercises may each need DIR filings. Cost rises with the number of shareholders, and a 100% parent-subsidiary result may become impossible. Overseas counsel is required. [Blog, Allen 2025-05-02, above]
- Rationale: an opinion piece by a VC lawyer (黃沛聲, 2023-12-29) argues that a US holdco reduces acquisition friction (US acquirers otherwise face 投審會 approval). [Secondary] https://meet.bnext.com.tw/articles/view/51007

Gaps: no Taiwan government or law-firm source gives a full flip step list, DIR approval times for flips, or fee ranges (legal, tax or filing). Costs remain unverified.

## 4. 臺灣科技新創基地 TTA, Taipei base admission criteria

**Result: corrected / new. TTA does not publish startup admission criteria for Taipei because the Taipei base admits startups only through the accelerator partners it selects each year. TTA South runs the direct residency calls.**

- About page: TTA is a 國科會 (NSTC) program launched in 2018. HQ / Taipei base: 臺北市松山區南京東路四段2號 (the Taipei Arena). Startups become TTA alumni in three ways: (1) selection by a TTA Accelerator Partner, (2) residency at TTA South (台南沙崙, 4,290 m², focused on smart tech, precision medicine and clean & circular tech), or (3) TTA exhibition programs. No direct startup admission criteria, fees or durations are listed for Taipei. Space and equipment fees are on request. [Primary] https://www.taiwanarena.tech/about/
- 2026 call for accelerators (2026 TTA產業型加速器進駐合作專案): posted 2025-12-05, applications due 115-01-19 17:00. Detailed rules are in a linked Google Drive PDF, not read. [Primary] https://www.taiwanarena.tech/program-detail/CALLFORPROPOSAL_2026/
- 2026-02-23: 13 accelerators selected: ATH Ventures, AVA Angels x AWS, BE Accelerator, FET Startup Accelerator, flyingVest Ventures, Foodland Ventures, HAOSHI, IAPS, Mosaic Venture Lab, Sustainable Impact Capital, SparkLabs Taiwan, STARTUP101, United Innovation. [Primary] https://www.taiwanarena.tech/program-detail/2026TTAAccelerator_PartnershipProgram/
- Practical implication: a startup wanting the Taipei base applies to one of these accelerators' own programs, whose criteria vary by accelerator.
- TTA South: the 12th residency round results were posted 2026-11-17 (11 teams; spaces range from 18–20 ping private offices to co-working seats; move-in within 45 days of lease start). The 11th round was 2026-05-05 (10 teams). The results pages do not restate criteria. The known South criteria (設立未滿8年 etc.) come from earlier call documents and were not re-checked this round.
