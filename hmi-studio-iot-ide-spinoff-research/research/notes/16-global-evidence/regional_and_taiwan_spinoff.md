# Regional market differences for embedded IoT/HMI tools, and Taiwan software spin-off mechanics (EDT HMI Studio → independent low-code IoT IDE company)

> Scope note: researched 2026-11-20. This is research material, not legal or tax advice. Items marked **[未驗證 / unverified]** come from the researcher's background knowledge and were NOT confirmed by a source fetched in this session. They are kept out of "Cited Findings" for that reason. Before the spin-off goes ahead, a Taiwan lawyer and CPA should confirm every law article cited here.

## (A1) China: domestic embedded GUI/IoT tool ecosystem, pricing, and whether a foreign paid tool can work

### Takeaway
Chinese embedded GUI tools are usually given away free to sell chips, boards, modules or cloud activations. ZLG says outright that its AWTK designer is "not how we make money, only how we attract customers". Revenue comes from per-device cloud licences (Tuya and Alibaba style) or from hardware. A foreign seat-licence IDE would face a free-by-default market, so China is a weak direct-revenue market. It is better treated as a supply-chain partner market (boards, modules) than as a market for selling licences.

### Cited Findings
- AWTK, ZLG's cross-platform embedded GUI engine, is open source under LGPL. Official docs say it can be used in commercial software without payment, and that it is "open source and free for commercial use" — [AWTK FAQ (ZLG)](https://awtk.zlg.cn/docs/awtk_docs/FAQ/1.AWTK.html); [GitHub zlgopen/awtk](https://github.com/zlgopen/awtk)
- Developers worried that LGPL might not allow statically linked embedded use. ZLG replied on a 2019 forum thread that it would provide a free commercial licence on request — [WhyCan forum: 求教，awtk是免费的吗](https://whycan.com/t_5688.html)
- AWTK Designer needs a login and a licence. A new account receives 3 licences valid for 2 months, and renewal requires "renewal coupons" that are only issued after the user completes a profile and passes a review. The designer stops working once the licence expires — [AWTK Designer User Manual §3 许可](https://awtk.zlg.cn/docs/awstudio_docs/AWTK_Designer_User_Manual/3.Licence.html)
- A ZLG representative said informally in 2020 that the designer is free: "AWTK开源，设计器免费。我们不靠它赚钱，只靠它吸引客户" (AWTK is open source and the designer is free; ZLG does not make money from it and uses it only to attract customers). Another user suspected advanced features might later be limited to customers. This is an informal forum statement, not a price list — [WhyCan forum](https://whycan.com/t_5688.html); summary context [知乎: AWTK Designer 再升级](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/356589748)
- RT-Thread Studio, the IDE from the RT-Thread RTOS project (company: 睿赛德), was launched about 2020 with a community version described as "free forever". It supports the full STM32 line, ST-Link and J-Link — [Embedded Computing Design: RT-Thread Studio](https://embeddedcomputing.com/technology/software-and-os/ides-application-programming/rt-thread-studio-ide-now-available-for-open-source-iot-rtos); [PR.com press release](https://www.pr.com/press-release/808913); listed as an ST partner product: [ST.com](https://www.st.com/ja/partner-products-and-services/rt-thread-studio.html)
- RT-Thread founder 熊谱翔 said in an interview that RT-Thread runs on more than 2.5 billion devices, making it the most widely installed domestic embedded OS, with developers in 70+ countries and regions — [观察者网 via 凤凰网](https://news.ifeng.com/c/8jCGykOulxm)
- Tuya sells cloud "licence / activation codes" per device as a product on its platform, needed for a device to activate on Tuya IoT cloud. Unit prices are not public — [Tuya support centre](https://support.tuya.com/cn/help/_detail/K8sdy16gicd9t); G2 also says Tuya does not publish developer-platform pricing — [G2 Tuya pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/tuya-iot-development-platform/pricing)
- Reference price point for the Chinese per-device cloud model (Alibaba Cloud's 生活物联网平台, not Tuya): RMB 2.80 per non-Bluetooth device (base pack with 100 messages), RMB 1.60 per Bluetooth device, RMB 5.00 per consumer camera — [Alibaba Cloud help: 激活码计费](https://help.aliyun.com/zh/document_detail/150384.html); [Alibaba Cloud help](https://help.aliyun.com/zh/document_detail/167594.html)

### Inferences
- The dominant Chinese pattern is that the tool is free and money comes from silicon, boards or cloud. ZLG (designer free to attract hardware customers), RT-Thread (free IDE, an ecosystem play) and Tuya and Alibaba (per-device activation of roughly RMB 1.6–5) all follow it. A foreign paid design-time seat licence would compete against free tools that come bundled with local silicon and local-language support. A per-device runtime or cloud fee is the model Chinese buyers already accept, at very low unit prices.
- Opportunity in China: run an HMI Studio-style tool on Chinese MCUs (ESP32, WCH, GigaDevice, Artery) and with Chinese board makers (Guition/JC "CYD" boards, DWIN). This is mainly useful for reaching the global hobbyist and long-tail OEM channel that buys those boards, not for selling licences inside China.
- **[未驗證 / unverified, background]** Espressif (ESP-IDF, ESP RainMaker), DWIN (DGUS tool, bundled free with its serial screens), NXP GUI Guider (free, LVGL-based), and OpenHarmony / LiteOS-M on MCUs are all free tools tied to hardware or ecosystems. This supports the same inference, but no source for them was fetched this session.
- Any China-made tool or component inside a US-bound connected product may become a compliance problem (see A4). That gives a Taiwanese vendor a "non-PRC supply chain" selling point in the US and EU.

### Gaps
- Current (2026) AWTK Designer pricing or paid tiers. The only sources found date from 2019–2021.
- Tuya per-device licence unit prices (not public) and Tuya developer membership tiers.
- Guition/JC, DWIN, WCH, GigaDevice and Artery tool pricing. Persimwear and RT-Thread commercial package pricing. RT-Thread (睿赛德) funding. None were found in this session.
- Whether any foreign paid embedded GUI tool (Qt, TouchGFX, SquareLine, Embedded Wizard) has meaningful paid adoption inside China. No data found.

## (A2) Japan, Korea, Southeast Asia: notable tools and buying behaviour

### Takeaway
In Japan, foreign GUI tools are sold through authorised domestic distributors and agents: SEGGER emWin through Envitech, Crank Storyboard through Osaka NDS. There is also an established domestic HMI-tool vendor, HI Corporation's exbeans UI Conductor, with consumer-electronics design wins (Epson printers). A Taiwanese entrant should expect to sign a Japanese distributor or trading company rather than sell direct. No sourced findings were obtained for Korea or Southeast Asia.

### Cited Findings
- HI Corporation (エイチアイ) sells exbeans UI Conductor, an HMI development tool for embedded and automotive devices. It lets designers preview screens, UI parts, animations and app interactions on a PC. V1.9.0 (Nov 2018) added view-models and a view-state editor — [MONOist 2018-11-28](https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/1811/28/news085.html)
- exbeans UI Conductor was used in Epson Colorio EP-879A printers (announced 2016). Screens edited in the tool move directly to the target — [MONOist 2016-11-02](https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/1611/02/news027.html)
- SEGGER emWin is distributed in Japan by 株式会社エンビテック (Envitech). Through Renesas it is offered as a GUI solution free for mass production on RX and RA MCUs — [Renesas JP: SEGGER emWin](https://renesas.com/ja/document/prb/segger-emwin)
- Crank Storyboard is sold in Japan through Osaka NDS (大阪エヌデーエス) as the official sales agent, with further authorised resellers below it — [IPROS product listing](https://mono.ipros.com/product/detail/2000859227)
- Japanese B2B catalogue IPROS has a dedicated "User Interface Development" category listing embedded GUI tools (e.g., SDTech) — [IPROS category](https://mono.ipros.com/en/cg3/User%20Interface%20Development/); [SDTech on IPROS](https://pr.mono.ipros.com/en/sdtech/product/detail/2001523956)

### Inferences
- Japan's route to market runs distributor → MCU vendor partnership (e.g., Renesas) → OEM. Silicon vendors make GUI middleware "free for production" on their own MCUs (emWin on Renesas), so a Taiwanese tool would probably need Renesas-ecosystem support plus a Japanese agent (an Osaka NDS-type firm) to be credible. Expect localisation (Japanese docs and support) and long qualification cycles. **[推論 / inference]**
- **[未驗證 / unverified, background]** Other Japan-relevant HMI tool vendors include Candera (CGI Studio, automotive) and Rightware (Kanzi). Rightware is owned by China's ThunderSoft, which matters for the US connected-vehicle rule in A4. Not verified this session.

### Gaps
- Korea: no source found on domestic embedded GUI tools or buying behaviour (e.g., Samsung/LG ecosystems, Korean distributors).
- Southeast Asia: no source found. The manufacturing base (Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia) is mostly contract manufacturing, where tool decisions are often made by the brand owner abroad. This is an inference only.

## (A3) EU: CRA and RED delegated act as demand drivers, and the industrial HMI market

### Takeaway
CRA vulnerability and incident reporting has applied since 11 Sep 2026. Full obligations, including machine-readable SBOMs, apply from 11 Dec 2027. Fines for reporting breaches reach €15M or 2.5% of worldwide turnover. Every connected-device maker selling into the EU therefore needs SBOM generation, vulnerability handling and update (OTA) capability built into the product lifecycle, and an IDE that produces these by default has a concrete regulatory hook for 2027.

### Cited Findings
- The CRA entered into force on 10 Dec 2024 and will be fully applicable on 11 Dec 2027. Open-source steward reporting obligations also start 11 Dec 2027 — [TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/cybersecurity/feature/EU-Cyber-Resilience-Act-reporting-What-CISOs-need-to-know); [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/cyber-resilience-act-major-shift-eu-cybersecurity-compliance)
- Since 11 Sep 2026 (Art. 14), manufacturers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents affecting products with digital elements. The EU single reporting platform was scheduled to go live the same day — [European Commission: CRA reporting](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/bel/legal-updates/ready-respond-report-the-cyber-resilience-act-s-reporting-regime-is-here); [Travers Smith](https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/the-eu-cyber-resilience-acts-vulnerability-and-incident-reporting-requirements-are-now-live/)
- Reporting is not retrospective. If active exploitation of a known vulnerability is discovered after 11 Sep 2026, the reporting clock starts at that point — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/bel/legal-updates/ready-respond-report-the-cyber-resilience-act-s-reporting-regime-is-here)
- Breaches of the reporting obligations can be fined up to €15M or 2.5% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/bel/legal-updates/ready-respond-report-the-cyber-resilience-act-s-reporting-regime-is-here)
- Machine-readable SBOMs become mandatory on 11 Dec 2027. Manufacturers must identify and document components, including an SBOM in a machine-readable format — [Sonatype](https://www.sonatype.com/blog/the-cra-reporting-deadline-is-almost-here?hs_amp=true); [Bootlin: CRA obligations for manufacturers](https://bootlin.com/blog/cyber-resilience-act-cra-obligations-for-manufacturers/)
- MCU vendors already publish CRA vulnerability-handling and reporting guidance for their customers (Microchip) — [Microchip online docs](https://onlinedocs.microchip.com/oxy/GUID-72031DB3-5FCC-44C9-9944-75A35D247D97-en-US-1/GUID-A875D3A7-4405-4943-9224-3D9B1D046910.html)
- Further law-firm guidance from Sep 2026 confirms reporting is live — [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/09/get-ready-reporting-faqs-and-checklist); [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/cyber-resilience-act-11-september-2026-key-starting-point-reporting-obligations); [Ireland NCSC CRA page](https://ncsc.gov.ie/cra/)

### Inferences
- The obligations bite on the *manufacturer*, i.e. the IDE's customer. That makes "SBOM export plus a vulnerability-tracking hook plus a signed OTA pipeline" a feature customers can justify in a budget, rather than a nice-to-have. The 11 Dec 2027 date gives a sales window of about 12 months from today.
- Small EU OEMs (the long tail building LVGL or MCU products) are the least able to build CRA tooling themselves, so they match a low-code IDE spin-off well.
- **[未驗證 / unverified, background]** RED Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 has applied to internet-connected radio equipment since 1 Aug 2025, with harmonised standards EN 18031-1/-2/-3. It adds requirements on network protection, personal data and fraud for wireless products, which covers most ESP32-class devices. Not re-verified with a fetched source this session.

### Gaps
- No source fetched on Germany's industrial HMI market size or buyers (Siemens, Beckhoff, Phoenix Contact, etc.).
- No fetched source on the RED delegated act (see the unverified note above).
- No IoT Analytics regional split for embedded tool spending.

## (A4) US: market structure, FDA §524B, Cyber Trust Mark, China-related restrictions

### Takeaway
US policy is steadily favouring non-PRC supply chains. The Cyber Trust Mark's first lead administrator (UL) withdrew over China ties and was replaced by ioXt in April 2026. The BIS connected-vehicle rule bans PRC- and Russia-linked vehicle software from Model Year 2027. FDA §524B requires SBOMs and cybersecurity plans for "cyber devices". A Taiwanese, non-PRC tool vendor that ships SBOM and secure-update support can position for medical, automotive-adjacent and consumer-IoT OEMs selling into the US.

### Cited Findings
- **Cyber Trust Mark:** the program was paused in June 2025 over concerns that the lead administrator, UL Solutions, had a joint venture with a Chinese firm and testing sites in China. UL withdrew in Dec 2025 — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/firm-overseeing-fccs-cyber-trust-mark-program-withdraws-over-ties-to-china); [SC World](https://www.scworld.com/brief/ul-withdraws-from-overseeing-iot-labeling-program)
- The FCC opened a new lead-administrator application window in Jan 2026 and selected ioXt Alliance on 13 Apr 2026, framing the choice around national security — [Global Policy Watch](https://www.globalpolicywatch.com/2026/01/fcc-opens-application-window-for-new-cyber-trust-mark-program-lead-administrator/); [Nextgov 2026-04](https://www.nextgov.com/cybersecurity/2026/04/fcc-selects-ioxt-alliance-lead-cyber-labeling-program/412800); [Broadband Breakfast](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/fcc-picks-ioxt-alliance-to-head-cyber-trust-mark-program/)
- On 27 Oct 2026 the FCC sought comment on implementation, including a physical label with a QR code and rules for keeping certification after authorisation. The FCC commissioner hopes labelled products reach shoppers within a year, so the mark is not yet on shelves as of Nov 2026 — [Cooley 2026-11-10](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-11-10-fcc-advances-us-cyber-trust-mark-program); [Nextgov 2026-10](https://www.nextgov.com/cybersecurity/2026/10/fcc-commissioner-hopes-cyber-trust-mark-products-reach-shoppers-within-year/416878/)
- **BIS connected-vehicle rule (ICTS):** final rule issued 16 Jan 2025, effective 17 Mar 2025. Software prohibitions (vehicle connectivity and automated driving systems with a PRC or Russia nexus) take effect for Model Year 2027. Hardware prohibitions take effect for MY2030, or 1 Jan 2029 for units without a model year. Declarations of Conformity are required — [BIS press release](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/commerce-finalizes-rule-secure-connected-vehicle-supply-chains-foreign-adversary-threats); [BIS connected vehicles page](https://www.bis.gov/connected-vehicles); [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/bis-connected-vehicles-rule-effective-as-of-march-17-2025/)
- The rule covers passenger vehicles only. BIS said it would pursue a separate rulemaking for commercial vehicles. Agricultural equipment and RVs are excluded — [Thompson Hine](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2025/01/bis-publishes-final-rule-prohibiting-the-sale-and-importation-of-connected-vehicles-with-sufficient-nexus-to-china-and-russia/); [Perkins Coie](https://perkinscoie.com/insights/update/new-us-commerce-prohibitions-chinese-and-russian-connected-vehicle-technology)
- **FDA §524B:** FD&C Act §524B(b)(3) requires an SBOM in marketing submissions (510(k), PMA, HDE, etc.) for "cyber devices" — [RAPS](https://www.raps.org/resource/fda-premarket-cybersecurity-guidance-clarifies-sbo.html); [FDA Cybersecurity FAQs](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/cybersecurity-medical-devices-frequently-asked-questions-faqs)
- On 27 Jun 2025 the FDA finalised "Select Updates for the Premarket Cybersecurity Guidance: Section 524B", added as Section VII of "Cybersecurity in Medical Devices: Quality System Considerations and Content of Premarket Submissions" — [Federal Register 90 FR (2025-06-27)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-06-27/html/2025-11669.htm); [King & Spalding](https://www.kslaw.com/insights/articles/fda-finalizes-premarket-cybersecurity-guidance-for-medical-devices); [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/fda-finalizes-cyber-device-select-updates-guidance)
- The FDA reads "cyber device" broadly as any device that contains software or is software. Manufacturers are expected to build SBOM generation and management into the quality system and supplier agreements — [King & Spalding](https://www.kslaw.com/insights/articles/fda-finalizes-premarket-cybersecurity-guidance-for-medical-devices); [Morgan Lewis 2025-11 (FCA enforcement angle)](https://www.morganlewis.com/de/blogs/asprescribed/2025/11/from-vulnerability-to-violation-fda-cybersecurity-requirements-for-medical-devices-and-fca-enforcement)

### Inferences
- "Supplier agreements must cover SBOM" (FDA) and "no PRC-nexus software in vehicle connectivity systems" (BIS) both make the *origin and auditability of the tool and runtime* part of procurement. A Taiwan-incorporated (or US-flipped) company with a clean, non-PRC ownership history can sell on this. Ownership history matters, so a spin-off cap table that excludes PRC investors keeps this option open. **[推論]**
- The BIS rule targets VCS and ADS software in passenger cars, not dashboard HMI tooling in general. It is a signal and an adjacent opportunity, not a direct mandate for HMI tools. **[推論]**
- **[未驗證 / unverified, background]** Kanzi (Rightware) is owned by PRC-based ThunderSoft. US automotive and medical customers might see that as a supply-chain risk, which would open room for non-PRC alternatives. Needs verification.

## (B1) Taiwan legal routes for the spin-off: 分割, 技術作價入股, IP assignment vs licence, employee transfer, 競業禁止

### Takeaway
There are two main legal routes. (1) A company split (分割) under 企業併購法. It is defined in 第4條第6款, needs board and shareholder approval under 第35–37條, has a creditor-objection period and joint liability, retained employees keep their seniority (第16–17條), and it carries tax benefits (第39條: stamp, deed and securities-transaction tax exemptions when ≥65% of consideration is voting shares; 第44條: corporate income tax exemption when ≥80% is shares). (2) A technology-as-capital contribution of the HMI Studio IP into a NewCo. Under 產業創新條例 第12條之1, tax on the shares received can be deferred until they are sold, for transfers or licences of self-developed IP, through 2029-12-31. Post-employment non-competes for staff who do not move are capped at 2 years and need 4 conditions (勞動基準法 第9條之1).

### Cited Findings
**企業併購法 (latest amendment 2022-06-15, 民國111年6月15日)** — [全國法規資料庫 企業併購法](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0080041)
- 第4條第6款: 「分割」 means a company transfers all or part of an *independently operable* business (得獨立營運之一部或全部之營業) to an existing or newly formed company, for shares, cash or other property.
- 第35條: the board prepares a split plan for the shareholders' meeting. The quorum is two-thirds of issued shares present, with approval by a majority of votes present (第2項). A public company that misses this quorum may instead meet with a majority of issued shares present and approve by two-thirds of votes present (第3項). A higher threshold in the articles prevails (第5項). After the resolution, creditors must be notified and given at least 30 days to object (第6項). The receiving company is jointly liable for the split company's pre-split debts up to the contributed amount, and that claim lapses 2 years after the split base date (第7項). In a *新設分割* (split into a new company), the split company's shareholders' meeting acts as the promoters' meeting and can adopt the articles and elect directors (第8項).
- 第36條: if the business is split into an *existing* company and its value is ≤20% of net worth with all consideration going to the split company, the board can approve it (2/3 of directors present, majority vote) without a shareholders' meeting. A similar board-only path applies when the receiving company issues ≤20% new shares or pays ≤20% of net worth (第2項). There is also a provision for a split company that is the sole shareholder of a new company (第3項).
- 第37條: a parent and a ≥90%-held subsidiary may split by board resolutions alone.
- 第16條: the receiving or new company must give retained employees written notice of their terms at least 30 days before the base date. Employees have 10 days to reply, and no reply counts as consent. **Retained employees' seniority must be recognised.** 第17條: employees who are not retained, or who decline, are terminated by the pre-deal employer with notice under 勞基法 第16條 and receive severance or pension. 第15條: the retirement reserve pays non-retained employees first, and the balance moves to the new company's reserve account.
- 第39條第1項: if voting shares make up ≥65% of total consideration, contracts and deeds are exempt from stamp duty, real-estate deed tax is exempt, securities transaction tax is exempt, the transfer of goods or services is outside business tax (營業稅), and land value increment tax can be recorded and deferred. 第39條第2項: if the split company sells shares within 3 years so that it holds less than 65% of the original consideration, the deferred land value increment tax becomes payable.
- 第44條: when a business or assets are transferred for shares making up ≥80% of consideration and the shares are distributed in full to shareholders, the gain is exempt from corporate income tax (第1項). Similar treatment applies to splits (第3項). Related articles: 第40條 (goodwill amortised over 15 years), 第40條之1 (intangible assets), 第41條 (M&A expenses over 10 years), 第45條 (consolidated tax filing for ≥90% subsidiaries).

**產業創新條例 (技術作價 deferral)** — [全國法規資料庫 產業創新條例](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0040051)
- 第12條之1第1項: individuals, companies and limited partnerships may deduct up to 200% of R&D spending from income arising from transferring or licensing their *own* IP. Companies must choose between this and the 第10條 R&D credit.
- 第12條之1第2項: anyone who *transfers (讓與) or licenses (授權) self-developed IP to a company for its own use* and receives new shares may elect to defer tax on those shares until they are actually transferred. Conditions include reporting; if income or cost is undocumented, a 30% cost deduction applies. The election cannot be changed once made — [MODA sample declaration](https://www-api.moda.gov.tw/File/Get/adi/zh-tw/sS8Q5fkBA6FXbzu)
- 第12條之1第3項 (individuals only): if the individual holds the shares *and* provides services related to applying that IP to the issuing company for a cumulative 2 years, the taxable amount is the lower of market value when acquired and the sale price. According to KPMG, this preferential base does not apply when companies or limited partnerships contribute self-developed IP — [MODA sample declaration](https://www-api.moda.gov.tw/File/Get/adi/zh-tw/sS8Q5fkBA6FXbzu); [KPMG e-Tax alert 125 (2019)](https://kpmg.com/tw/zh/home/insights/2019/06/e-tax-alert-125.html)
- 第72條: 第12條之1 and 第12條之2 apply from 2020-01-01 to **2029-12-31 (民國118年12月31日)** — [全國法規資料庫](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0040051)
- Without deferral, the gain from technology-as-capital is taxed in the year the shares are received (up to 40% for individuals), which creates a cash tax on illiquid shares — [EY Taiwan: technology contribution strategies for pre-IPO startups](https://www.ey.com/zh_tw/insights/tax/pcs-technology-contribution-strategies-for-pre-ipo-startups)
- 第19條之1 (employee share compensation deferral, up to NT$10M a year, 2-year service condition). The page lists an application period of 2027-01-01 to 2029-12-31 after an amendment. The database showed the act's latest amendment as 民國115年9月30日 (2026-09-30). **[Flag: amendment date read from the database page via a summarising fetch tool; verify the 沿革 page.]** — [全國法規資料庫](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0040051)

**勞動基準法 第9條之1 (post-employment non-compete)** — [全國法規資料庫 勞基法 §9-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=9-1)
- Valid only if all four conditions hold: (1) the employer has a legitimate business interest to protect; (2) the worker's position gives access to or use of trade secrets; (3) duration, region, scope of activity and target employers are within a reasonable range; (4) the employer pays reasonable compensation (which excludes pay received during employment). A clause failing any one condition is void. The **maximum is 2 years**; anything longer is cut to 2 years.

### Inferences
- **Route choice for EDT (an unlisted or listed display-module maker):**
  - *Route 1: 新設分割 (企併法).* EDT splits the HMI Studio business into a new 100%-owned NewCo, then sells NewCo shares to the team and investors. Advantages: employees move with seniority intact (第16條), tax exemptions apply (第39條, 第44條), and it is a clean legal transfer of the whole business (contracts, IP, staff). Disadvantages: HMI Studio must be an "independently operable business" (第4條第6款); a shareholders' meeting is needed unless the 第36條 or 第37條 shortcuts apply; and there is 2 years of joint liability for pre-split debts up to the contributed amount (第35條第7項). This is the same mechanism AUO used for 達擎 and AMS (see B3).
  - *Route 2: EDT incorporates NewCo, or a founder team incorporates it, and EDT contributes the HMI Studio IP (讓與 or 授權) as capital.* 第12條之1第2項 lets the contributor defer tax until it sells the shares. Simpler, and suits a small software unit, but employees must resign and be rehired (seniority is not automatically carried over), and the IP must be valued (an appraisal report is normally needed for 技術作價). **[未驗證 / unverified, background]** 公司法 第156條第5項 permits shares paid with technical know-how, subject to board resolution. Not fetched this session.
  - *Route 3: exclusive licence plus a cash or share deal.* EDT keeps IP ownership and grants NewCo an exclusive worldwide licence. 第12條之1第2項 also covers 授權. Investors usually dislike this: VCs prefer that NewCo *owns* its core IP, and a US flip or later M&A will require assignment. **[推論]**
- Non-compete: for EDT staff who *do not* join NewCo, or for NewCo founders under EDT agreements, any non-compete is limited to 2 years and needs compensation. **[未驗證 / unverified, background]** 勞基法施行細則 第7條之3 sets a minimum compensation of 50% of the average monthly wage. Not verified this session. More practically, the spin-off agreement should carry explicit carve-outs so that former EDT staff working at NewCo are not caught by EDT non-compete clauses.
- The first-party sources do not settle whether a *company* (EDT) contributing IP under 第12條之1第2項 still gets deferral. They indicate yes ("individuals, companies, partnerships"), but the 第3項 preferential base does not apply to companies. Tax advisor confirmation is needed.

### Gaps
- Exact text of 公司法 第156條第5項 and of the board and appraisal requirements for 技術作價 (not fetched).
- 營業秘密法 implications for code and know-how moving with the team (not researched).
- Whether 企併法 第44條's ≥80%-shares income-tax exemption needs the shares to be distributed to EDT shareholders (第1項 wording suggests yes) or also fits a 分割 where EDT keeps NewCo shares. A CPA should confirm the exact 第44條第3項 wording.

## (B2) Taiwan funding sources and typical round sizes

### Takeaway
國發基金 (National Development Fund) 創業天使投資方案 doubled to NT$10B in Nov 2025. It now invests up to NT$100M in a single company (previously NT$20M) in startups up to 8 years old, and accepts applications through 2030-12-31. 產創條例 第23條之2 gives individual angels a 50% income deduction. In 2025 the median Taiwan seed round was about US$1.01M and the median early-stage (A/B) round about US$3.31M.

### Cited Findings
- In Nov 2025 the National Development Fund's 創業天使投資方案 was expanded from NT$5B to NT$10B. The single-company cap rose from NT$20M to up to NT$100M, eligible company age was extended from 5 to 8 years, and acceptance runs to 2030-12-31. One report gave a different deadline, so check the official notice — [聯合報 2025-11](https://udn.com/news/story/7238/9115341); [聯合報](https://udn.com/news/story/7238/9115215); [優分析](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/1722737467)
- The programme has run since 2018-05-22 and had approved 296 startups by Oct 2025. By end-2025 the cumulative total was 299 companies and NT$4.305B invested — [聯合報](https://udn.com/news/story/7238/9115341); [聯合報 (applications >2,800)](https://udn.com/news/story/7238/9184086)
- 產業創新條例 第23條之2: an individual's cash investment of ≥NT$500,000 in one company in one year, in a certified high-risk startup under 8 years old, held 3 years, can be deducted at 50% from comprehensive income. The annual cap is NT$5M, of which no more than NT$3M can go to companies outside the national key industries. Applies through 2029-12-31 — [全國法規資料庫 產業創新條例](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0040051)
- In 2025 Taiwan startup investment totalled US$2.774B across 555 deals, averaging about US$5M per deal. Funding concentrated in health, biotech, energy, hardware and industrial manufacturing — [經濟部中小及新創企業署 press release](https://wwwcdn.sme.gov.tw/article-tw-2276-15174)
- FINDIT 2026 annual report (2025 data): the median seed-stage round (pre-seed, seed, angel) was **US$1.01M**, the median early-stage (A, B) round **US$3.31M**, and the median late-stage round US$8.08M. Seed made up 34.8% of deals and early stage 55.3% — [FINDIT 2026 臺灣新創投資趨勢年報 總覽篇](https://findit.sme.gov.tw/tw/Res/2693)
- Software examples from 2025: Zeabur (cloud deployment) raised a US$2M angel round, 賦語科技 a NT$25M seed, and 鑫蘊林科 (AI software) a US$35M Series A — [FINDIT 2025 Nov–Dec](https://findit.org.tw/tw/Res/2540); [FINDIT 2025 Sep–Oct](https://findit.sme.gov.tw/tw/Res/2519)

### Inferences
- A realistic financing plan for an EDT software spin-off: EDT plus founders put in IP and seed cash; a seed round of about US$1M (near the national median) is co-led by a Taiwanese VC or CVC; the 國發基金 天使方案 matches angel or VC money (it invests alongside qualified co-investors, see Gaps); individual angels use the 第23條之2 deduction. **[推論]**
- Because Taiwan capital goes mostly to hardware, biotech and energy, a pure B2B dev-tool company is off-trend locally. That raises the importance of foreign or CVC investors (MCU vendors, distributors) and an eventual US flip. **[推論]**

## (B3) Taiwan precedents and lessons on going global

### Takeaway
Large Taiwanese electronics groups routinely carve units into 100%-owned subsidiaries by 分割 and later list or sell part of them. AUO did this for 達擎 (2021), AUO Mobility Solutions (2026) and its energy unit (2026); Acer has turned many units into separately listed companies. Taiwan software firms that went global (Gogolook, Appier) did it by going abroad from day one: Southeast Asia and Japan first, hiring local managers, and listing overseas (Appier on the Tokyo exchange). No source was found on Advantech WISE-PaaS being spun off.

### Cited Findings
- **AUO → 達擎:** in May 2020 AUO's board approved splitting its general and public-information display business into 100%-owned 達擎 (AUO Display Plus), effective 2021-01-01. It was AUO's first spin-off of a business unit; 達擎 handles R&D, sales and service while AUO keeps manufacturing — [TechNews 2020-06-17](https://finance.technews.tw/2020/06/17/auo-passed-the-daqing-division-base-day-january-1-2021/)
- **AUO → AUO Mobility Solutions (AMS):** in Nov 2024 the board approved moving the smart-mobility business group and BHTC into a new 100%-owned subsidiary, base date 2026-01-01 — [自由時報](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/breakingnews/4871165)
- **AUO → 達耀能源:** in Apr 2026 the board approved splitting the energy business into 100%-owned 達耀能源科技, base date about 2026-08-01, along with a restructuring and disposal of about NT$860M in 星耀投控 — [Newtalk 2026-04-13](https://newtalk.tw/news/view/2026-04-13/1029575); [聯合報](https://udn.com/news/story/7240/9439594)
- **Acer:** 智聯服務 (Acer e-Enabling Service Business) was split from Acer in Sep 2017 and later listed. In 2020 Acer split its smart air-quality business into an existing subsidiary (AGI) and also planned to list 宏碁資訊 (Acer ITS). 宏碁資訊 targeted an OTC listing in Q3 2022. By Nov 2023 Acer had 11 listed subsidiaries, with 宏碁智通 among those planned for IPO — [工商時報 2020-05-07](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20200507700227-430502); [數位時代](https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/60715/acer-group-esg-policy-ast); [旺得富 2022-07-14](https://wantrich.chinatimes.com/news/20220714900297-420101); [鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/4534161); [今周刊 2020-05-06](https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/80393/post/202005060076/)
- **Gogolook (Whoscall):** the founder said from day one that the company had to go abroad. Thailand's monthly active users overtook Taiwan's, and the company planned dual Taipei–Bangkok HQs. It hired local Southeast Asian managers to make up for Taiwanese startups' weak local networks and uncompetitive pay. It built public-private credibility by working with Taiwan's police (刑事局) and the FSC on scam calls. NAVER bought a majority stake, later released it, and Gogolook went on to IPO — [TechNews (Gogolook IPO)](https://technews.tw/?p=1086155); [數位時代 topic](https://www.bnext.com.tw/topic/view/806); [天下](https://www.cw.com.tw/article/5140149)
- **Appier:** listed in Japan (Tokyo Stock Exchange), an early example of a Taiwanese startup listing abroad. 2021 revenue was ¥12.7B (+41% YoY) — [TechNews](https://technews.tw/?p=360981); [中央社 2025-01-31](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202501310025.aspx)

### Inferences
- The 分割 into a 100% subsidiary first, then outside investment or listing, is a well-trodden path in Taiwan's display and PC industries (AUO, Acer). EDT's board and auditors will recognise it, which lowers execution risk compared with a novel structure. **[推論]**
- Differences from those precedents: they were hardware or services units sold mostly to existing customers. A dev-tool spin-off must build a global self-serve and developer channel (English docs, community, online payments), which the Gogolook and Appier cases suggest needs local market leadership hires and possibly an overseas listing or entity. **[推論]**
- Typical difficulties going international (pricing in USD per seat or per device, English support, distributor channels in Japan, investor preference for a US Delaware parent or "flip") are plausible but **not sourced in this session**. See Gaps.

- Gigabyte, Moxa, Delta or MediaTek-backed software spin-offs: not researched for lack of tool budget.
