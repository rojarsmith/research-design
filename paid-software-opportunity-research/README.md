# What kinds of software are worth charging for

Research date: 2026-11-03. The full report is in [README.zh-TW.md](README.zh-TW.md); this page is a short overview. It follows the [Markdown editor market research](../markdown-editor-market-research/README.md).

## Summary

- Software that sells usually solves a problem tied to money, deadlines or regulation for buyers with a budget. Free tools and quickly AI-generated utilities also have to fall short. Generic personal tools such as editors, notes, to-do lists and converters fail all three tests.
- Criteria:
  - the buyer is a business or a professional
  - the pain is tied to money, deadlines or compliance
  - free alternatives are inadequate
  - the value is ongoing
  - there is a barrier AI cannot clear in one shot
  - the builder has an edge
- Categories that tend to sell:
  - vertical B2B workflows
  - plugins for major professional software
  - hardware-adjacent tools
  - compliance tools
  - system integrations
  - private or on-premises AI
  - local gaps in the Taiwan market
- Recommended direction from this repository's research: Bitdove OTA plus EU CRA compliance. CRA vulnerability and incident reporting applies from 2026-09-11, and most product obligations apply from 2027-12-11.
- Before building embedded or HMI products personally, check employment IP and non-compete terms, because HMI Studio is an employer product.

The categories have not been checked for market size, competitors or customer demand.
