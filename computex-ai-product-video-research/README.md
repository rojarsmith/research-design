# Computex AI product video production

Research date: 25 October 2026. [Full Traditional Chinese plan](README.zh-TW.md).

This project plans an EDT HMI Studio product video for a roughly 50-inch Computex display, with simultaneous English and Traditional Chinese captions and both 4K and 1080p deliverables. Claude is intended to direct production through MCP-connected cloud services: planning, asset generation, timeline assembly, rendering and revisions.

The proposed pilot uses Creatomate for composition, ElevenLabs for audio subject to tool availability and commercial terms, and optional Runway footage. Shotstack is the alternative compositor. Firefly music is a manual export fallback unless a supported connector is verified. Actual product screens and hardware demonstrations remain captured evidence.

Start with a 20-second integration test before producing the proposed 90-second main video and 30-second loop. The event year, budget, final voice language, display orientation, product build and CTA remain open. The schedule provisionally references the officially announced 1–4 June 2027 event.

Documents cover tool research, MCP orchestration, bilingual storyboards, source assets, claim verification, audiovisual specifications, budget, schedule and release checks. No accounts have been connected and no video has been rendered. Platform documentation has been researched; integration and output quality still require the pilot.

The [feature and cost assessment](research/04-feature-cost-budget.zh-TW.md) adds official subscription prices, Taiwan Firefly pricing, usage calculations, monthly versus annual commitments, and pilot/production/ongoing scenarios. A provisional single-operator software allowance is NT$2,000 for a 1080p pilot and NT$8,000 per production month, excluding labor, equipment, extra taxes and custom enterprise music licensing. These scenarios allow a manual Firefly music export; an entirely MCP-driven music workflow needs a separate company-license quote.
